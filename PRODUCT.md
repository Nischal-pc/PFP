## Platform

## Users
Building owners, facility managers, and administrators responsible for multi-tenant commercial, industrial, healthcare, educational, and hospitality facilities in Western Canada.

## Product Purpose
PFP Canada provides professional fire protection services—including design, installation, monitoring, and certified inspections—to ensure life-safety systems meet regulatory compliance (NFPA, ULC, etc.) and building code requirements.

## Positioning
A single-source, reliable partner for bundled multi-system fire protection management (alarms, sprinklers, extinguishers, lighting, kitchen suppression), ensuring portfolio-wide compliance with automated scheduling and digital reporting.

## Operating Context
Clients manage properties with varying risk profiles (e.g., high-hazard industrial vs. healthcare "defend-in-place"). Services are delivered through certified technicians and integrated with digital compliance documentation for Authorities Having Jurisdiction.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Confirmed services: Fire Alarm Systems, Fire Extinguishers, Emergency & Exit Lighting, Kitchen Suppression, Sprinkler Systems, and Fire Protection Inspections. Compliance standards include NFPA, CAN/ULC, and National/Provincial Building Codes.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: PFP Canada (Legal Name: Precision Fire Protection Canada Ltd.)
- Tagline: "Fire protection you can trust, across Western Canada."
- Headquarters: Calgary, AB
- Service focus: Western Canada, 24/7 emergency support.

2. **Reliability & Trust:** 24/7 availability and certified expertise are central to the brand.
3. **Operational Clarity:** Simplify the management of complex, multi-system life-safety portfolios.
